Refunds and cancellation

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Cancelling

You can cancel Pro at any time in the app (Account → Plan and billing → Manage subscription). Cancelling stops the next renewal; Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and your account then returns to the free plan.

Refunds

  • If you're not happy with your first payment, ask for a refund within 14 days of it and you'll get it in full.
  • Renewals can be refunded within 14 days if you've used fewer than 20 hosted questions since the renewal.
  • If the service didn't work for you because of a fault on our side, ask us and we'll make it right.

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record; refunds go back to the original payment method through them. Consumer rights where you live, such as a right of withdrawal, apply in addition to this policy.
